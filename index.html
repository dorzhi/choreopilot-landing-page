ChoreoPilot

Your AI practice partner for Latin dance

Record yourself, get instant movement feedback, and train with a virtual leader or follower — starting with bachata.

Practice Latin social dance with AI

ChoreoPilot helps Latin social dancers practice alone with their phone camera. The app recognizes body movement, timing, posture, rhythm, and step patterns, then gives simple feedback and scoring so dancers can improve before class, practice, or social dancing.

How ChoreoPilot works

  1. Record your practice.
  2. Recognize movement with AI.
  3. Score timing, posture, balance, smoothness, and confidence.
  4. Practice again with a virtual leader or follower guide.

Record, recognize, score, and practice

Use your phone camera to capture a short attempt. ChoreoPilot detects body movement, measures key categories such as visibility, timing, side movement, weight transfer, posture, and smoothness, and gives one clear focus for your next attempt.

Starting with bachata. Built for more.

ChoreoPilot begins with bachata as the first practice module, then expands toward salsa, merengue, cha-cha, reggaeton, and other Latin social dance styles.

Practice with a virtual leader or follower

ChoreoPilot's virtual partner is a clean ghost silhouette that demonstrates timing, movement direction, and role-based rhythm for solo practice. Followers can train with a virtual leader guide. Leaders can train with a virtual follower guide. Beginners can practice solo with count guidance.

Who ChoreoPilot is for

  • For beginners learning timing.
  • For leaders building confidence.
  • For followers improving body movement and response.
  • For dancers practicing before socials.
  • For teachers who want students to practice between classes.

Frequently asked questions

Is ChoreoPilot only for bachata?

ChoreoPilot starts with bachata as the first practice module, but the long-term vision is to support Latin social dance styles such as salsa, merengue, cha-cha, reggaeton, and more.

Do I need a dance partner?

No. ChoreoPilot is designed for solo practice. You can train with a virtual leader or follower guide and use your phone camera to receive movement feedback.

What does the AI score measure?

The score can include timing, posture, rhythm, balance, smoothness, body movement, and practice consistency.

Can beginners use it?

Yes. ChoreoPilot is designed for beginners and improvers who want simple, clear feedback while practicing at home.

Does it work on mobile?

The product is designed around mobile recording, so dancers can practice with their phone camera.
